Rating drafts

After Claude reviews a Kimi, Gemini or OpenRouter draft, it records what happened to it: used (kept as-is), fixed (kept after edits) or discarded. You can ask for it too, e.g. “rate the last gem call discarded”.
To update this page, say usage in Claude Code. It also refreshes itself once a day at 09:00 while the Claude app is open.
